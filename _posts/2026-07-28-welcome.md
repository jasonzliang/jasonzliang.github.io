This is the first post on my blog. I'll use this space for working notes and
essays on the things I spend my days thinking about: **agentic reasoning,
inference-time compute, and large-scale neuroevolution** as a path toward AGI.

This post doubles as a template — it shows every kind of content the blog
supports, so future posts are copy-paste-and-edit.

def evolve(population, generations):
    for _ in range(generations):
        for net in population:
            net.train()            # inner loop: gradient descent
        population = select(population)  # outer loop: evolution
        population += mutate(population)
